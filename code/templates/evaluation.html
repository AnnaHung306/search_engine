Evaluation & review

Precision@5 uses frozen queries, one fixed collection and content-based relevance judgments. Missing rank positions count as nonrelevant; unjudged returned documents stop scoring.

New experiments require two independently completed human reviews and explicit adjudication of disagreements. The tool checks records and declared provenance; it cannot authenticate reviewers’ identities.
